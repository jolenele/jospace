Jo Space is inspired by MySpace and Jo (aka Josephine from Little Women).

_Thú thật hồi nhỏ chưa biết tên gốc nó là Little Women đâu, mà chỉ toàn gọi là Bốn cô con gái nhà bác sĩ March thôi LOL_

Jo has been my fav character since I was little. She is a strong and willful writer, who struggles to subdue her fiery temper and stubborn personality. That I see myself pretty much reflected in this characteristic. So I chose the name Jo as an inspiration to kick in my blog.
