title: 'Whiskey Sour'
---

_My fav cocktail the traditional Whiskey sour_

### Ingredients

3/4 ounce fresh lemon/orange juice (it’s always the best using fresh juice)

3/4 ounce simple syrup (or 1 small sugar cube with water)

1 egg white (optional)

2-ounce bourbon (or rye whiskey)

1 big cube of ice

Angostura bitters or lemon/orange slice

### Recipe Prep

Adding juice, syrup, and bourbon into a shaker.

If you are making a traditional whiskey sour, adding 1 egg white for the foamy top. Then dry shake vigorously until the egg white looks frothy. This step called dry shake since it has no ice in it.

If you don’t like the idea of egg white, you can skip the dry shake, add ice directly in and shake about 20 seconds until you feel the outside of the shaker is really cold.

Straining the mix through a Hawthorne strainer into either an old-fashioned or coupe glass.

Garnish with two lines of Angostura bitters, you can decorate it into a little feathered pattern. Or just simply garnish with a half lemon/orange wheels.

**Notice for Boston shaker:** If you comming from the foamy egg, remember to shake it softly first to cool down the temperature inside the shaker. When you feel it cool enough, you can create an airtight seal in the shake and really go for it. Or else you might have to do the cleaning.
